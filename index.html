What I do

I keep the University of Dayton's enterprise server fleet running: Linux and Windows, virtual and physical, patched through change management and covered around the clock by an on-call rotation I'm part of. Before that I spent more than a decade on the other side of the table, supporting clients across five industries, running my own IT practice, and managing operations for a small business.

What I'm looking for

A DevOps, systems administration, or systems architecture role, ideally with a consulting firm. I want to own infrastructure end to end, from provisioning and automation through the change management that keeps all of it boring in the best way. I work best where a technical problem is tangled up with a process problem, because I've spent most of my career on both sides of that line.
