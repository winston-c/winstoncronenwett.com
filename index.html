What I do

I'm a Systems Engineer at the University of Dayton, administering an enterprise fleet of more than 1,400 Linux and Windows hosts. I contribute to the Ansible and Terraform automation, delivered through GitLab CI/CD, that deploys, updates, and configures our 400+ Linux servers, and I built the self-hosted, GPU-accelerated AI node behind our AI-assisted development. Before that, I spent three years delivering IT for external clients and several more running operations for a small business.
