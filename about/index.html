About me

More than fifteen years of technical work, though not in a straight line.

The short version

I started behind the counter of a computer shop, studied operations management at Ohio State, ran operations for a small business, started my own IT practice, and then worked my way from client support into enterprise systems engineering. The operations years are why I think about process, cost, and the people using a system alongside the system itself.

Where I've worked

2025 — present

Systems Engineer

University of Dayton, UDit Enterprise Enabling Technologies

I administer the University's enterprise Linux and Windows servers, virtual and physical. Day to day that means patching through change management, watching security alerts and performance, and working with the other engineers to keep configurations standard across the fleet. I also build new systems alongside other UDit teams and take my turn on call.

2022 — 2024

IT Systems Analyst

University of Dayton, Department of Student Development

The project I'm proudest of here was the department's IT asset management system. I scoped it with stakeholders and built it, then later moved it onto Linux and Docker with no data loss, which made it much faster on hardware we already owned. It was recognized in the department's 2023 Impact Report. I also wrote the cross-department standards that cut down on misrouted support tickets.

2021 — 2022

Client IT Support Specialist

Incito Solutions LLC, a managed service provider

I supported clients in higher education, finance, non-profit, medical, and manufacturing, often working directly with owners on urgent security, data recovery, and configuration problems. At one client, The Modern College of Design, I was the embedded first point of contact for IT and incident response.

2019 — 2021

Proprietor

A Plus PC Doctor

I ran my own IT services business: on-site and remote support, custom system builds, hardware repair, malware cleanup, and teaching clients how to keep themselves secure.

2017 — 2019

Operations and Strategic Planning Manager

DNA Computers LLC

I built and documented the sales, service, and operations processes for a small computer business using Kanban, which made our work more predictable and our margins better. I used store data to launch a house-brand PC line and upgrade packages that grew revenue, and I handled hiring for a team of up to five.

2014 — 2015

Operations Intern, Corporate

Speedway LLC

A corporate internship that put me in a store as assistant manager over a team of up to twelve. I also ran a regional compliance initiative, more than 600 checks across 40 stores in six months, and built the process for reporting the results to senior management.

2009 — 2013

Team Leader

DNA Computers LLC

My first job in IT: leading small teams through peak seasons, fixing hardware, software, and configuration problems, and training new hires.
